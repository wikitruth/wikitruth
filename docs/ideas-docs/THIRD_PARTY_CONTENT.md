# Third-Party Content Policy

Wikitruth may cite external research, reporting, court records, government
documents, screenshots, or educational material when it is useful context.
That does not imply permission to redistribute a complete copy.

For public repository content:

- Prefer a citation and source URL over a copied publication.
- Do not commit complete third-party documents or generated full-text
  conversions without a compatible license or written permission.
- Do not commit screenshots containing third-party usernames, profile images,
  comments, or other personal data merely as design inspiration.
- Record the author, source, license, and any required attribution when an
  external asset is intentionally included.
- Keep cloud-provider folder IDs, resource keys, import manifests, and private
  sharing URLs out of version control.

Project-owned notes, designs, and artwork should be kept separate from
reference material so their provenance is clear.
