* Ranking: entries having most gems rank better, screened rank better, posts from user or reviewer with good reputation rank better (vs users with bad reputation or no reputation), new posts with a lot of bronze gems ranks better as well.

## Truth & Skepticism

How to mitigate too much skepticism

1. Doubts/questions should have reasonable basis before being taken seriously.
2. Skepticism should be proportional on the importance and controversy of the topic.
   1. Questioning if the Earth is round should have valid argument or evidence that it is otherwise considering the vast evidences that it is so.

What is truth as defined by the system

1. The system does not claim that its status “truth” is absolute truth but truth that has passed a certain level of skepticism.
   1. If the system states the Earth being round as a baseline for truth then major clauses of Evolution that are highly subject for skepticism due to their counter-intuitive or being questionable by a major percentage of people may not be on the same level and thus fall under levels of likeliness e.g. “more likely”, “likely”, etc.
