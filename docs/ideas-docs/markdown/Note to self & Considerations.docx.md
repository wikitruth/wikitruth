Note to self & Considerations

**The system should be**

* Very user friendly
* Should not be frustrating otherwise people will not use it even how useful it is
* The system should not rely on external evidences outside the system that's why the Artifacts/Assets/Evidences section was decided. So that each supporting evidence can also be validated and debated.
* Focused on vital information mostly by collection of arguments (list) instead of large body of text.
* Empower the users as much as possible to lessen bottlenecks, if it can be done by regular contributor without peer reviewer then allow it.
* Allow the discussion feature embeddable by external sites
* Content contribution will primarily be by creating/updating arguments. Content blocks in topics should not be new data but should be based on arguments/evidences, it's just mainly to format it and make it friendly for laypeople.

**Keep in mind**

* Finding the truth is not by pure logic or evidence, it's a combination of both reason and evidence with analysis structured from detailed and high-level perspective, looking into the totality of data.
* Your main purpose for the system is not only to let people know the truth but to know the truth for your own self as well.
* You need to earn somehow, via ads, donations, funding or other means, if possible, to sustain the operation of the system and the people working in it (via a foundation entity?)
* The initial content will come from you, if the system does not attract users, you will continue adding content.
* The system will be effective in finding the truth because it processes small arguments where logic and evidences can be easily compared.
* The reason why people are convinced to believe something or they don't because they can't see the whole picture.
* Be careful not to let the system slip and become an encyclopedia. The system is not just a collection of information but a repository of human knowledge validated and contrasted with truth and reality.
* Establish the truth and validity of information based on reality even from the smallest and most basic knowledge if needed.

**What the system can be**

* A living book of science, continually evolving and encompassing what is part of science and what is not
* Like a morality book, that says the good things and bad things that you should do and not do.
* A comprehensive version of Snopes, for verifying what is truth and reality, a fact checker

**Other features**

* Contributor questionnaire
  + What is truth
  + Ways to find it
  + How people can manipulate it (lies, twisted philosophy, biases)

**Key Points**

* Break down big, complex and broad topics and arguments until it’s easy to discuss and agree upon.
* Weed out illogical arguments, misleading statements, word redefinition, logical fallacies, negative biases
* Minimize trust on authority
* Network and references of supporting arguments
* Zero duplicate policy
* Strict labelling of what is true and not through verdicts
* Separation of truth and moral arguments
* Strict and systematic controls of knowledge contribution
* Start on most important and controversial topics with most impact to the society.
* Inquiry on truth need not be time limited, discussions are perpetual, verdict may constantly change based on new facts.
* Enforce respectful argumentation
* Proportion of skepticism based on level of controversy of topics
* Knowledge that is traceable, open for scrutiny and open system of contribution
* Application of various ways to find out the truth - inductive and deductive reasoning, scientific method, critical thinking, creative thinking, careful conclusion and use of intuition, statistics, and probability
* Contrast everything to reality
* Importance of contextual meaning plus intent over literal interpretation of arguments
* Focus on the importance of intent and message when evaluating a statement, not on the words.
* Potential forms of unsubstantiated insults or scorn (lack of respect or unjustly offensive): satire, joke, sarcasm, irony
* Understand more beyond the word or sentence.
* Manage confusion, misinformation, wrong impression (no to clickbaits), sensationalism, fake news
* A system that runs like a synthetic neural network to produce an intuition-like capability in judging the truth and good.
