**Upvoting**

* "Let the world know" by upvoting, promoting of a truth/argument/reality
* Top voted will be shown first, on top of the list, Top items in Home, trending, Top Truth/Reality
* Top voted will alert peer reviewers and will be required to prioritize
* Helps in organizing content by gathering non-critical votes, user-consensus.

Peer Reviewer Votes

* The more support, arguments, verified comments in an item, the more peer review votes is needed to change it’s status
* Veteran peer reviewers can gain more power (more voting value) but won’t grant them super powers to override approval/voting. Just like 2x of normal votes or bigger multiplier. Or maybe not?
* How do users set they agree or are against an argument? Via upvote?
