Fundamental Rules

* Ultimately, based on empirical evidence and human reason, logic, critical thinking and thorough analysis.
* No duplicate argument, content. Do not repeat the same debate/argument.
* Logical, verified, with source (reason, logic exists)
* Contrasted to reality (indicated by levels of status)
* Concise, simplified to the highest level possible
* Clearly written, easily understood by laypeople
* Based on general human perception of reality
* Based on general human reason, logic (including common sense)
* Posts should be polite, morale/ethically appropriate
* Once a topic has had enough verified sources, a peer reviewer can nominate a classification thereby undergoing another approval.
* The system does rely on public assumptions, specially on information outside the system. Unchallenged facts may reside outside the system unless challenged, then it has to be put in the system for discussion and verification
* Very basic general information that is generally accepted, that is non-controversial and having a lot of support can be taken easily as fact unless otherwise challenged

* Tagging an item for status, category
