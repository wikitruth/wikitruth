**Acceptance & Verdict**

* More controversial topics require more accepted arguments before being accepted as true.
* More controversial topics requirement higher level of evidence verification
* Some generally accepted facts can be accepted easily even without substantial evidence unless otherwise questioned rationally.

Levels of Truth

* T1 - priori & a posteriori
* T2 - very much makes sense or generally accepted fact but may lack support or evidence is unverifiable
* Partial Truth - applies to Topics; when only partial parts of the umbrella topic is proven
