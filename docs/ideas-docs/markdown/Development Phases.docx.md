**High-Level Goals**

1. Facts presentation and arrangement
2. Facts gathering via collaboration/community contribution
3. Strict facts control re acceptance and presentation

**Core Technical Features**

* All entries should be unique
* Inline create/edit (refresh is ok)
* Inline expand/collapse of entries
* Verdict votes aggregation and ranking (vetted by reviewers)
* 1v1 debate via opinions/comments thread.
* List image preview
* Entry cover image support using an artifact
* Top entries in newsfeeds style - mixed type and sorted by edit date.

**Core Objectives (To achieve ASAP)**

* Derive big, bold conclusions from many small facts
  + Breakdown big topics into small easily agreeable facts
  + The derived big conclusions will be traceable and undisputed since they are fully supported by many small facts
* Insights and rich context
  + Discover, reveal hidden truths, eliminate confusing issues by aggregation, organization of simple facts and evidences
    - Surfacing systematic but hidden acts: Lies, Conspiracies, Corruption
    - Goodness
    - Patterns, Behaviors, Connections
  + Get instant and direct facts: easily see the truth from difficult, big, or complex topics without the need to spend time to understand or perform research
  + Present content for easy comprehension by wikipedia-level audience
* Dialectics
  + Produce an undisputable conclusion by brute force extraction of truth with the help of visualization, linking, bite-sized, and well-organized content
  + Positive, friendly, and well-balanced discussion
* Content zooming from trunks to roots and vice versa for
  + Outline
  + Visualization and mindmapping
* Integrity by traceability
  + Complete history tracking: edits, verdicts, reports/appeals
  + Clear structure and organization to surface truth
  + No deletion policy (screening with rejection)
  + Artifacts management
  + Proper labelling and tagging of content
* Surfacing and resolving social issues
  + Enforcing accountability
  + Quickly remind the society about forgotten issues, effective tracking
  + Surface the solution for the problems
* Elimination of noise in an ocean of data (internet)
  + Organize facts to showcase only what’s relevant, useful, and important
  + Eliminate trivial and unnecessary facts (noise)
