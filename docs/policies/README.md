# Content Operations Policies

These policies define deterministic operating rules for modern Wikitruth workflows. They complement the concise target-state canonical cards without changing them.

## Active Policies

- `DUPLICATE_AND_MERGE_POLICY.md`: duplicate scope, candidate scoring, moderator decisions, merge survivorship, redirects, and audit requirements.
- `SOURCE_QUALITY_RUBRIC.md`: source-quality dimensions, scoring, evidence requirements, and review thresholds.
- `MODERATION_PLAYBOOK.md`: contributor, screener, reviewer, and administrator responsibilities and escalation paths.
- `TRUTH_AND_ETHICS_VERDICT_POLICY.md`: independent factual and ethical verdict channels, evidence expectations, and unresolved states.

## Operating Rule

Automated checks may identify candidates and enforce explicit invariants, but they must not silently merge records, expire unresolved content, or convert an ethical judgment into a factual verdict. Human decisions must include a reason and produce a privileged audit event.
