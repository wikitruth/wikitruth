# Contributor And Reviewer Policy

Policy version: `1.0`  
Effective date: `2026-07-13`

## Contributor Standard

Contributors must search the intended parent scope before creating content, use the smallest independently reviewable claim, distinguish observation from inference, and attach source limitations. Titles must state the subject without advocacy language. Factual and ethical judgments must remain separate.

Prohibited submissions include impersonation, personal data without a public-interest basis, threats, spam, fabricated evidence, duplicate flooding, and edits intended to erase attributable history.

## Reviewer Standard

Reviewers decide from recorded evidence and policy, not contributor reputation or agreement with the conclusion. Every rejection, merge, verdict, critical-issue resolution, rollback, appeal, and override requires a reason. Conflicts of interest must be disclosed and reassigned.

Reviewers must preserve dissenting evidence, identify the policy version used, and keep factual and ethical verdict channels independent.

## Versioning

- Material policy changes increment the version and require renewed onboarding acknowledgement.
- Typographical clarifications may retain the version but must be dated in Git history.
- Existing decisions retain the policy version under which they were made.
- Administrators may not retroactively relabel historical decisions without a new audited review.
