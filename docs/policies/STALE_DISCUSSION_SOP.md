# Stale Discussion SOP

Version: `1.0` (`2026-07-13`)

## Trigger

A parent-entry revision automatically marks earlier revision-linked comments as potentially obsolete. This flag is a review queue, not a deletion or automatic archive action.

## Review Procedure

1. Compare the comment's recorded context revision with the current parent revision.
2. Mark `relevant` when the reasoning still addresses the current claim.
3. Mark `obsolete` when the changed text removes or materially alters the addressed claim.
4. Record a reason of at least ten characters and link the relevant revision where practical.
5. Preserve the comment and decision in history.

## Takeover And Archive

If an author is unavailable, another contributor may submit a change request; ownership is not silently reassigned. Threads may be archived only through an authorized human decision. There is no age-based deletion, automatic expiry, or cleanup job.
