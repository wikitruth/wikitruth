# Concise Writing Standard

Version: `1.0` (`2026-07-13`)

## Reviewable Content

- One entry should express one primary claim, question, answer, issue, observation, or evidence item.
- Put the conclusion in the title or first sentence.
- Prefer a short summary followed by evidence and limitations.
- Split independent claims instead of joining them with broad narrative.
- Replace rhetorical labels with observable descriptions.

## Five-Second Check

A reader should be able to identify the subject, claim type, current review state, and why it matters within five seconds. If that is not possible, narrow the title and add an at-a-glance summary.

## Enforcement

Screeners return entries that are materially ambiguous, duplicative, or too broad to review. The return reason must identify a concrete rewrite, not merely say "too long." Existing long-form context may remain in artifacts or linked references rather than being destroyed.
