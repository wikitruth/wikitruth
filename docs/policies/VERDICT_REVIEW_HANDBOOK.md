# Verdict Review Handbook

Version: `1.0` (`2026-07-13`)

## Factual Review

- `supported`: reliable evidence directly supports the bounded claim.
- `refuted`: reliable evidence directly contradicts the bounded claim.
- `mixed`: material parts are supported and contradicted.
- `insufficient_evidence`: available evidence cannot support a conclusion.
- `pending`: no final reviewed decision exists.

Reviewers must identify the claim, relevant date, strongest supporting and contradicting evidence, source-quality limitations, and reasoning. A final factual decision cannot proceed while an accepted critical issue directly blocks the target unless an administrator records a reasoned override.

## Ethical Review

- `permissible`, `impermissible`, `contested`, `not_applicable`, or `pending`.

The reasoning must name the ethical framework or principle. Ethical approval does not prove a factual claim, and factual support does not determine permissibility.

## Calibration

For contested or high-impact decisions, two reviewers independently assess the evidence before discussing differences. Calibration records the disagreement category: evidence selection, source quality, claim scope, policy interpretation, or ethical framework. The final decision retains minority reasoning when it remains material.
