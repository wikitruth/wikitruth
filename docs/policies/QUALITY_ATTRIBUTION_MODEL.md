# Quality Attribution Model

Version: `1.0` (`2026-07-13`)

## Principle

Recognition follows durable quality signals, not popularity alone. Scores remain formula-versioned and explainable; they do not determine factual or ethical truth.

## Credited Work

- Accepted, non-duplicate contributions.
- Evidence with complete provenance and strong source-quality review.
- Accepted change requests and reasoned issue resolutions.
- Reviewer votes, appeals, and moderation decisions that survive later review.
- Stewardship such as maintaining links, revisions, and historical outcomes.

## Guardrails

Rejected spam, duplicate flooding, self-reaction, and administrative access do not create quality credit. Negative scores cannot silently remove permissions. Formula changes create a new snapshot version and preserve prior calculations for auditability.

Raw vote volume and raw privileged-action volume do not create stewardship credit. Review work becomes durable only after the configured durability window and is excluded or reversed when the associated decision is overturned.

Profiles may display dimensions, badges, and evidence counts. Ranking may use these signals only as one transparent input alongside screening state, relevance, recency, and public reactions.
