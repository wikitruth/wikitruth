# Moderation Playbook

## Contributor

- Search within the intended parent scope before creating an entry.
- Use a precise title and separate factual claims from ethical judgments.
- Attach provenance and state source limitations.
- Respond to change requests through suggestions rather than overwriting protected content.

## Screener

- Confirm entry type, parent scope, basic readability, and visibility boundary.
- Review duplicate candidates before acceptance.
- Reject spam, unsafe content, and records with no recoverable purpose.
- Escalate verdict questions and contested merges to a reviewer.

## Reviewer

- Apply the source-quality rubric and record reasoning.
- Keep factual and ethical verdict channels independent.
- Resolve change requests, stale conflicts, merge previews, appeals, and critical issues.
- Use rollback only from a reviewed revision and state why the prior state is being restored.

## Administrator

- Manage roles, policy configuration, recovery, and integrity verification.
- Do not substitute administrative access for evidence review.
- Investigate broken audit chains, redirect cycles, repeated override behavior, and unauthorized private/public boundary changes.

## Service Targets

- Duplicate candidate review: before screening acceptance.
- Critical issue acknowledgement: within one operating day.
- Appeal acknowledgement: within two operating days.
- Stale change request: return to author with the conflicting revision identified.

These targets are operational goals, not automatic expiry rules. Content remains unresolved until an authorized human decision is recorded.

## Required Decision Record

Every screening rejection, verdict, merge, rollback, appeal resolution, and policy override must include actor, timestamp, target, old state, new state, reason, and related evidence or revision identifiers.
