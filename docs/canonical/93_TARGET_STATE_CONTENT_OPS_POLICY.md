# Canonical Card: Target State Content Ops and Policy

## Purpose

Define target governance and content-operations assets required for quality at scale.

## Policy and Enablement Targets

- Versioned contributor and reviewer policy packs.
- Issue taxonomy handbook with moderation examples.
- Verdict policy handbook aligned with UI labels and review practice.
- Duplicate/merge policy and decision rubric.
- Source quality rubric and reviewer evidence checklist.

## Operational Workflow Targets

- Screening playbook with SLA expectations.
- Reviewer escalation and appeal playbook.
- Stale discussion cleanup and archive SOP.
- Concise-writing standard with enforceable checklist.
- Quality-linked incentives and attribution metrics model.
- Unified knowledge-health queues for evidence gaps, unresolved critical
  issues, quorum gaps, revalidation, source failures, duplicates, and important
  unanswered questions.
- Non-destructive freshness policies that schedule review and notify owners or
  followers without expiring content automatically.

## Seed and Curation Targets

- Seeded flagship topic structures with full evidence/discussion scaffolding.
- Starter-topic workflows that label curation targets for contributors.
- Template-driven topic/category seeding and maintenance model.

## Target State Status

- Policy and enablement assets: `implemented` and exposed through the in-app policy center.
- Operational handbooks and the seed-curation playbook: `implemented`.
- Unified knowledge-health and non-destructive freshness queues: `implemented`
  and locally verified.
- Real-world flagship seeding, reviewer calibration, and sustained curation remain content operations, not missing software implementation.
- Source baseline: `docs/plans/deferred/plan-2026-04-14/08_VALIDATED_CHECKLIST_CONTENT_FIXPH.md` and `docs/system-reference/07_REFERENCE_LIBRARY_AND_EVIDENCE_POLICY.md`.
