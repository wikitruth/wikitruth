# Canonical Card: Target State Overview

## Purpose

Capture the intended future-state direction for Wikitruth, including unimplemented goals.

## Target Product Intent

- Be a structured truth-validation platform for high-impact and controversial topics.
- Make claim status clear for readers: verified, unresolved, disputed, outdated.
- Keep evidence and reasoning traceable, not author reputation-driven.

## Target Principles

- Decompose large topics into small reviewable units.
- Preserve context chains so claims are not interpreted in isolation.
- Separate truth-evaluation from ethics/value-evaluation.
- Maintain transparent lifecycle and governance for major decisions.

## Target State Status

- This card is an active directional product contract. Governance, traceability, lifecycle, and truth-versus-ethics foundations are implemented, while channel-specific consensus hardening, durable reputation calibration, content quality, and operating adoption are active work under the 2026-07-18 epistemic-kernel plan.
